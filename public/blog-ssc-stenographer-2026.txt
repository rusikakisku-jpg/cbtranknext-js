  <h2 style="font-size: 20px; font-weight: bold; color: #0f172a; margin: 28px 0 12px; border-bottom: 2px solid #e2e8f0; padding-bottom: 8px;">1. SSC Stenographer 2026 Written Exam Pattern &amp; Marks Distribution</h2>
  <p style="color: #475569; margin-bottom: 16px;">According to Para 13 of the official advertisement (F. No.: E/13/2026-C-2), the Computer Based Examination (CBE) consists of <strong>200 Objective MCQ Questions (200 Marks)</strong> with an exam duration of <strong>120 Minutes (2 Hours)</strong>. Each correct answer carries <strong>1.0 Mark</strong>, while <strong>0.25 (1/4th) Mark</strong> is deducted for every incorrect response. Sectional timers are implemented: 30 minutes each for Part I &amp; II, and 60 minutes for Part III.</p>
