⚖️ 3. Official Negative Marking & Score Calculation Formula

As stipulated in CEN 09/2025 notification, the raw score is calculated using the standard negative marking formula:

Net Raw Score = (Total Correct Answers × 1.0) − (Total Wrong Answers × 0.3333)
Note: Unattempted questions carry 0.00 marks (no penalty).
