  <h2 style="font-size: 20px; font-weight: bold; color: #0f172a; margin: 28px 0 12px; border-bottom: 2px solid #e2e8f0; padding-bottom: 8px;">1. SSC CHT 2026 Paper-I Exam Pattern &amp; Marks Distribution</h2>
  <p style="color: #475569; margin-bottom: 16px;">According to Para 11 of the official advertisement (Notice: Combined Hindi Translators Examination, 2026), Stage-I consists of <strong>Paper-I Computer Based Examination (CBE)</strong> featuring <strong>200 Objective MCQ Questions (200 Marks)</strong> with an exam duration of <strong>120 Minutes (2 Hours)</strong>. Each correct question awards <strong>1.0 Mark</strong>, while <strong>0.25 (1/4th) Mark</strong> is deducted for every incorrect response. Note that sectional timers of 60 minutes (1 hour) each are applicable for General Hindi and General English.</p>

  <h2 style="font-size: 20px; font-weight: bold; color: #0f172a; margin: 28px 0 12px; border-bottom: 2px solid #e2e8f0; padding-bottom: 8px;">4. Official SSC CHT Negative Marking Formula</h2>
  <div style="background-color: #f8fafc; border: 1px dashed #cbd5e1; border-radius: 8px; padding: 14px 16px; margin: 14px 0 20px; font-family: monospace; font-size: 14px; color: #0f172a;">
    Paper-I Net Raw Score = (Total Correct Answers &times; 1.0) &minus; (Total Wrong Answers &times; 0.25)<br>
    <span style="font-size: 12px; color: #64748b;">Note: Unattempted questions receive 0 marks (no deduction). In multi-shift exams, raw scores are normalized according to the official SSC formula published on 02-06-2025 to determine final cut-offs.</span>
