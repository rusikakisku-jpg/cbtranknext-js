What is CBT RANK and How Does It Work?

CBT RANK is an online analytics platform designed to help competitive exam students evaluate their performance immediately after an examination. When recruitment boards release answer key links, our system reads the HTML response sheet, matches candidate responses against official keys, and generates an authentic score card.

Key analytics provided include:

  • Total Questions Overview: Clear count of attempted, unattempted, correct, and incorrect questions.
  • Net Raw Score: Exact calculated score after factoring in positive marks and negative penalty deductions.
  • Section-Wise Performance: Subject-by-subject marks for Quantitative Aptitude (Mathematics), General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness (GK/Current Affairs), English Language, and General Science.
  • Live Rank Matrices: Real-time All-India rank, shift rank, category rank, and gender-wise standing.

Multi-Level Rank & Shift Difficulty Prediction

Because major national exams are conducted across dozens of shifts over multiple weeks, question difficulty naturally varies. Knowing only your raw marks does not show where you stand in merit. CBT RANK solves this by providing:

  • All-India Overall Rank: Shows your position compared to all participating candidates nationwide.
  • Shift-Wise Rank & Average Marks: Compares your marks against students from your exact exam date and shift to measure paper difficulty.
  • Community / Category Rank: Evaluates your rank within your specific reservation category, including UR (General), OBC, EWS, SC, ST, PwBD, and Ex-Servicemen.
  • Gender-Wise Rank: Segregates ranks for male and female candidates for exams with separate gender quotas or physical standards.

How to Check Your Score on CBT RANK (Step-by-Step)

  1. Access Official Portal: Visit your exam commission login portal and log in using your Roll Number / Registration Number and Password.
  2. Open Response Sheet: Click on the link for "Candidate Response Sheet / Tentative Answer Key".
  3. Copy URL: Copy the complete web address from your browser URL address bar.
  4. Paste into CBT RANK: Navigate to cbtrank.com/answerkey and paste the link into the designated field.
  5. Submit & View Results: Select your category and state, then click Submit to view your instant marks report, correct/wrong question summary, and live rank.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is CBT RANK completely free to use?
Yes, CBT RANK is 100% free for all competitive examination candidates across India without any subscription or payment requirement.
Is it safe to paste my answer key URL on cbtrank.com?
Yes. CBT RANK only analyzes the publicly generated HTML markup of the response sheet to count correct and incorrect answers. We do not ask for or store passwords or sensitive private login credentials.
Can I modify positive and negative marks manually?
Yes. The platform auto-detects standard marking rules, but you can manually adjust values to reflect specific recruitment guidelines.
How accurate is the shift rank and category rank?
The rankings update dynamically as candidates submit their response sheets. With thousands of authentic submissions per exam, the percentile and shift difficulty indicators provide a reliable benchmark ahead of official results.
