<h2>What is CBT RANK and How Does It Work?</h2>

<p>
  <strong>CBT RANK</strong> is an online analytics platform designed to help competitive exam students evaluate their performance immediately after an examination. When recruitment boards release answer key links, our system reads the HTML response sheet, matches candidate responses against official keys, and generates an authentic score card.
</p>

<p>Key analytics provided include:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Total Questions Overview:</strong> Clear count of attempted, unattempted, correct, and incorrect questions.</li>
  <li><strong>Net Raw Score:</strong> Exact calculated score after factoring in positive marks and negative penalty deductions.</li>
  <li><strong>Section-Wise Performance:</strong> Subject-by-subject marks for Quantitative Aptitude (Mathematics), General Intelligence &amp; Reasoning, General Awareness (GK/Current Affairs), English Language, and General Science.</li>
  <li><strong>Live Rank Matrices:</strong> Real-time All-India rank, shift rank, category rank, and gender-wise standing.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Multi-Level Rank &amp; Shift Difficulty Prediction</h2>

<p>
  Because major national exams are conducted across dozens of shifts over multiple weeks, question difficulty naturally varies. Knowing only your raw marks does not show where you stand in merit. CBT RANK solves this by providing:
</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>All-India Overall Rank:</strong> Shows your position compared to all participating candidates nationwide.</li>
  <li><strong>Shift-Wise Rank &amp; Average Marks:</strong> Compares your marks against students from your exact exam date and shift to measure paper difficulty.</li>
  <li><strong>Community / Category Rank:</strong> Evaluates your rank within your specific reservation category, including <strong>UR (General), OBC, EWS, SC, ST, PwBD, and Ex-Servicemen</strong>.</li>
  <li><strong>Gender-Wise Rank:</strong> Segregates ranks for male and female candidates for exams with separate gender quotas or physical standards.</li>
</ul>
